NUMSTEP: TORUS

Taurus rules

Taurus follows the same numbered-chain idea as Classic, but the grid is wrapped into a torus.

  • Start a chain on a coloured clue.
  • Follow the numbers in order.
  • Move one square horizontally or vertically at a time.
  • Diagonal moves are not allowed.
  • Black squares are blocked and a square cannot be reused.
  • As in Classic, a highest-numbered final clue can be a terminal finish marker.

Adjacency: The left edge joins the right edge, and the top edge joins the bottom edge. A move off one edge therefore re-enters on the opposite edge. The board has no boundary in those two directions, so an edge cell can have neighbours that appear on the opposite side of the displayed grid.
