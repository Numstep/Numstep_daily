Who runs Numstep?

Numstep is an independent puzzle site operated by Ben Cornish. For questions about this site, privacy, copyright or sponsorship, contact numstep@proton.me.

Cookies and similar technologies

Numstep uses a small amount of browser storage and cookies to provide basic site functionality and to remember your privacy choice. Essential storage is not used to create an advertising profile.

Optional analytics is separate from the core puzzle. It is not loaded on a new visit unless you choose to accept analytics. You can reject analytics and still use the site normally.

Analytics

If you choose to accept analytics, Numstep uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, including which pages and puzzle variants are visited. The Google Analytics measurement ID used by the site is G-BWBT7DYH8Z.

Numstep's Analytics configuration disables Google Signals and advertising personalisation. Google may process analytics information as a service provider. Google's own privacy information applies to its processing.

Visitor counter

The site may display a visitor counter supplied by Visitor Badge. It is treated as optional measurement and is only requested after you have accepted analytics. The counter is intended to provide an approximate site-visit figure rather than identify individual visitors.

Changing your cookie choice

The Cookie settings button is kept at the bottom of the site so that you can change your choice later. Your choice is stored for a limited period and can be changed at any time.
