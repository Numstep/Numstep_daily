NUMSTEP CLASSIC

Classic rules

Complete every numbered chain by moving through adjacent white squares in numerical order.

  • Start a chain on its coloured clue.
  • Move horizontally or vertically: up, down, left or right.
  • Diagonal moves are not allowed.
  • Black squares cannot be used.
  • A square cannot be visited twice.
  • Each chain ends immediately before the next clue.
  • If the final clue is the highest number, it is a terminal finish marker; you win when the preceding chain reaches the number immediately before it.

Adjacency: Classic uses ordinary four-way grid adjacency. Only the four sides of a square count as neighbours; the grid does not wrap around its edges.
