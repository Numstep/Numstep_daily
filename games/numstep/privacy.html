Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Who is responsible for Numstep?

Numstep is operated by Ben Cornish. For privacy questions, contact numstep@proton.me.

What information does Numstep collect?

Numstep is designed to work without requiring an account or asking you to provide personal information. The site does not currently have user accounts or a player database.

If you contact Numstep by email or through the contact form, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your message.

Essential storage

The site may use browser storage that is necessary for basic functionality, such as remembering your cookie-consent choice. This is not used to build an advertising profile.

Google Analytics

With your permission, Numstep uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-BWBT7DYH8Z) to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages and puzzle variants are viewed. Analytics is optional and is not loaded on a new visit unless you choose to accept analytics.

Google may process analytics information on Numstep's behalf. Google's own privacy information and terms apply to that processing. Numstep has disabled Google Signals and advertising personalisation in its Analytics configuration.

Site visitor counter

Numstep also has a sitewide visitor badge supplied by Visitor Badge. It is treated as optional measurement: the badge is not requested unless you have accepted analytics. The badge uses the same sitewide counter rather than a page-by-page counter.

Future advertising

Numstep does not currently use Google AdSense. If advertising is introduced, this notice and the consent controls will be reviewed and updated before advertising technologies are enabled.

Your choices

You can accept or reject optional analytics when you first visit the site. You can change your choice later using the Cookie settings control. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from playing Numstep.

Retention and sharing

Numstep keeps information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Email enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry and maintain appropriate records. Numstep does not sell personal information.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. To exercise a relevant right or ask a privacy question, contact numstep@proton.me.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the site, analytics, advertising or account features change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
