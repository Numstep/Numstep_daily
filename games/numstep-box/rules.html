NUMSTEP: BOX

Box rules

Box wraps the numbered-chain puzzle around the six faces of a box.

  • Start a chain on a coloured clue.
  • Follow the numbers in order.
  • Move between cells that share an edge on a face, or across a box edge where the two surface cells meet.
  • Do not move diagonally across a face or through the box.
  • Black cells are blocked and a cell cannot be reused.
  • The final highest clue can be a terminal finish marker, so the preceding chain may be the winning chain.

Adjacency: Box is a surface graph rather than a flat grid. Each cell has four surface neighbours in the puzzle's explicit adjacency map. At the edge of a face, the appropriate neighbouring cell on the adjoining face becomes adjacent. The corners and face transitions therefore matter: cells that look far apart in the flattened representation can be immediate neighbours on the box, while cells that merely appear close in the drawing are not necessarily adjacent.
