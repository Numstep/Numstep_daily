NUMSTEP: CUBE

Cube rules

Complete every chain by moving through the cube in numerical order.

  • Start a chain on a coloured clue.
  • Move to a cell sharing a face with the current cell.
  • Moves may be within the same layer or between two adjacent layers.
  • Diagonal moves, including moves that only touch at an edge or corner, are not allowed.
  • Black cells are blocked.
  • A cell cannot be visited twice within a chain.
  • The final highest clue can be a terminal finish marker; the preceding chain completes the puzzle.

Adjacency: Cube uses three-dimensional six-way face adjacency. A cell can have up to six neighbours: above, below, left, right, forward and backward. Crossing from one displayed layer to the next is a normal move when the cells share a face.
